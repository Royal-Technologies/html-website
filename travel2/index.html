The Road To The Summit

Expedition Process

Rigorous pre-trip screening, equipment vetting, and expert leadership every step of the way.

Phase 01

Register Online

Submit your application, physical fitness profile, and mountaineering background for review by our head guide.

Phase 02

Gear Briefing

Receive a personalized equipment audit, rental kit packing list, and join a 1-on-1 virtual conditioning consultation.

Phase 03

Expedition Launch

Meet your guide crew at base camp, run equipment safety drills, acclimatize safely, and begin the summit push.

Phase 04

Summit & Certificate

Stand atop the summit ridge, celebrate with your expedition team, and receive your official IFMGA achievement badge.
