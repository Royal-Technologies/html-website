About RDS

RDS is a non-profit development organization, committed to poverty reduction, food security, gender equality and sustainable livelihoods for the under-served communities of Bangladesh. Since 1992, RDS has been working with the poor and marginalized people to create lasting change through participatory and inclusive development.
