Dental Trauma Emergency Protocol

Follow these immediate measures if you experience dental or facial trauma:

  • Knocked-Out Adult Tooth: Keep tooth moist in cold milk or saliva. Do not scrub the root. Arrive within 60 minutes for re-implantation.
  • Severe Facial Swelling: Indicates an active spreading infection. Contact our on-call trauma surgeon immediately.
  • Broken Orthodontic Wire: Cover sharp end with orthodontic wax. Do not cut wire yourself.
