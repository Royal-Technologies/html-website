The Movement

Where Sound Meets Tomorrow's Technology

Founded in 2017, SoundStage Live has grown into the world's most anticipated hybrid festival. We unite electronic music pioneers with tech innovators, digital artists, and creative technologists.

Spread over 120 waterfront acres, the festival is 100% carbon-neutral, powered by zero-emission solar battery generators, and operates a zero-single-use-plastic policy.
