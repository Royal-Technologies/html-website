19+ Years of Conservation
Ancient Wilderness

The Lungs of Our Living Planet

Tropical rainforests harbor over 50% of Earth’s terrestrial biodiversity and act as colossal carbon sinks. Yet every minute, an area equivalent to 10 soccer fields is cleared by slash-and-burn operations.

EcoRoots Earth works tirelessly to buy back threatened border concessions, confer legal land titles to ancestral tribes, and re-wild barren pastures.

  • Strict permanent conservation easements preventing future development
  • Zero chemical pesticides or invasive monocultures planted
  • Transparent satellite mapping updated quarterly on our public dashboard
  • Direct local employment generating sustainable regenerative economies
Lifecycle of Restoration

How We Re-Wild Deforested Land

A meticulous scientific nursery process taking tiny seeds into towering centenary canopy.

1

Native Seed Harvesting

Forest rangers gather organic seeds from deep inside ancient mother trees.

2

Community Nursery

Seedlings are nurtured for 8 months in solar shade nurseries until root-hardened.

3

Monsoon Planting

Planted across targeted wildlife corridors during peak rainfall seasons for 90%+ survival.

4

Satellite Stewardship

Monitored via optical satellites and ranger audits for 5 continuous years.
