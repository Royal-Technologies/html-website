Know Your Rights If Arrested

Follow these golden rules to avoid compromising your defense:

  • Do Not Answer Questions: Politely state: "I am invoking my right to remain silent and I want my attorney present."
  • Do Not Consent to Searches: State clearly: "I do not consent to any search of my person, phone, vehicle, or home."
  • Demand Your Phone Call: Contact ApexTrial Advocates immediately before answering any interrogation questions.
