Who we are

Overview of CREED

Centre for Rehabilitation Education Earning Development (CREED) is a registered voluntary non-governmental development organization established in 1987. Founded by Mohammad Sulaiman Khan alongside dedicated social workers, CREED envisions an exploitation-free, self-reliant society where underprivileged families live with dignity, self-respect, and economic security.

CREED operates with full regulatory compliance under licenses from the Microcredit Regulatory Authority (MRA License No. 01079-03171-00358), NGO Affairs Bureau (Reg. No. 805), Department of Social Services (Reg. No. Dha-02293), and Registrar of Joint Stock Companies & Societies.

Read More

Since our inception in 1987, CREED has been firmly devoted to alleviating poverty and nurturing self-reliance among underprivileged and marginalized families in Bangladesh. True empowerment emerges when grassroots communities gain access to financial capital, practical education, safe drinking water, and agricultural support.

Operating through 21 branch offices across Munshiganj, Narayanganj, Cox's Bazar, and other regions, our disciplined microcredit programs have disbursed over ৳362.8 Crore with an exemplary 98.71% recovery rate. In tandem, our dedicated primary school in St. Martin Coral Island has provided free schooling to more than 1,400 vulnerable children over the last 23+ years.

We extend our deepest gratitude to the Microcredit Regulatory Authority (MRA), NGO Affairs Bureau, government agencies, development partners, and our diligent field workers. Above all, we salute our resilient beneficiaries whose relentless dedication turns hope into enduring progress.

— Mohammad Sulaiman Khan, Founder & CEO, CREED
What we do

Our Eight Pillars

CREED works to improve the lives of marginalized communities across Bangladesh through eight key areas of sustainable development.

Inclusive Finance

Expanding access to financial services for low-income and marginalized communities.

  • Jagoron & Buniad microcredit
  • Financial literacy training
  • Women-led village samities
  • 98.71% recovery discipline

Enterprise Development

Promoting small and micro enterprises to strengthen local rural economies.

  • Aogrosor enterprise loans
  • Access to business capital
  • Market linkage support
  • Tailoring, carpentry & trades

Animal Husbandry (AHDP)

Livestock development and cattle fattening for household income security.

  • Cattle fattening projects
  • Veterinary care & vaccination
  • Dairy farm inputs & training
  • Livelihood resilience

Food Security (GFSC)

Campaigning for global and local food security and sustainable agriculture.

  • Global Food Security Campaign
  • Sustainable crop farming
  • Homestead vegetable gardens
  • Nutrition for rural families

Island Primary Education

Providing free formal education to vulnerable children on St. Martin Island.

  • 23+ years continuous schooling
  • Over 1,400 students educated
  • Free books, bags & stationery
  • Dedicated island teachers

Tube-Well & Safe Water

Ensuring access to clean, safe drinking water across underserved communities.

  • Deep tube-well distribution
  • Arsenic-free drinking water
  • Waterborne disease reduction
  • Community maintenance support

Ultra-Poor Graduation

Lifting the ultra-poor from extreme vulnerability through integrated support.

  • Buniad program coverage
  • Productive asset transfers
  • Subsistence safety nets
  • Step-by-step graduation

Disaster Relief & Sahos

Strengthening community resilience against economic and environmental shocks.

  • Sahos emergency financial loans
  • Post-flood recovery support
  • Emergency livelihood aid
  • Resilient community networks
