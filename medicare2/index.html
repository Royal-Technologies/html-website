Our Procedures

Advanced Cardiac Procedures

From minimally invasive interventions to complex open-heart surgeries, our specialists deliver exceptional outcomes.

Coronary Bypass Surgery

CABG restores blood flow by bypassing blocked arteries using grafts from the patient's own vessels, significantly reducing heart attack risk.

Learn More

Angioplasty & Stenting

Minimally invasive balloon angioplasty with stent placement to open blocked coronary arteries and restore normal blood flow with rapid recovery.

Learn More

Pacemaker Implantation

Advanced single, dual, and biventricular pacemaker implantation to regulate heart rhythm and treat bradycardia and heart block conditions.

Learn More

Valve Replacement

Surgical or transcatheter (TAVR) aortic, mitral, and tricuspid valve repair and replacement using mechanical and biological prosthetics.

Learn More

Heart Transplant

Comprehensive heart transplant program including pre-transplant evaluation, surgery, and lifetime post-transplant care with a dedicated team.

Learn More

Cardiac Rehabilitation

Medically supervised cardiac rehab program combining exercise training, education, and counseling to accelerate recovery and prevent future events.
