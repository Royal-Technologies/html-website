Safety First

Zero Compromises on Electrical Safety

Faulty wiring and overloaded breaker panels are among the leading causes of residential fires. VoltShield Electric enforces strict safety protocols and precision thermal imaging diagnostics on every project.

Our work is backed by a 10-year craftsmanship warranty and guaranteed to pass all municipal and insurance safety inspections on the first visit.

  • State licensed master electricians on every site

  • Complete municipal permit acquisition and city sign-offs
