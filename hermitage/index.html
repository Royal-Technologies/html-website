The Process

How Certification Works

Our streamlined 4-step process gets your organisation certified quickly, with dedicated support from experienced auditors every step of the way.

1

Application

Submit your enquiry form. We assess your scope, timeline and certification requirements, then provide a fixed-fee quotation within 24 hours.

2

Gap Analysis & Audit

Our certified auditors conduct a thorough Stage 1 document review followed by a Stage 2 on-site or remote audit of your management system.

3

Certification Issued

Upon successful audit, your internationally recognised certificate is issued within 5 working days, valid for 3 years with annual surveillance.

4

Surveillance & Renewal

Annual surveillance audits maintain your certification currency. We provide ongoing support to ensure continual improvement and renewal readiness.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about certification timelines, costs and processes.

How long does ISO 9001 certification take?

Typically 8–16 weeks from initial application, depending on your organisation's size, complexity and existing documentation. Our fast-track service can reduce this significantly. We'll provide a tailored timeline after your free gap analysis.

Are Hermitage certificates internationally recognised?

Yes. Hermitage Certification Group is fully IAF accredited and UKAS recognised. Our certificates carry the IAF multilateral recognition arrangement (MLA) mark, making them accepted by regulators, governments and business partners in 100+ countries.

Can audits be conducted remotely?

Yes. We offer fully remote, hybrid and on-site audit options to suit your preference and business operations. Remote audits use our secure digital audit platform and are accepted for most standards including ISO 9001, ISO 27001 and GDPR.

What happens if we don't pass the audit first time?

Our 98% first-time pass rate reflects our thorough pre-audit preparation support. If non-conformities are found, we work with you to address them and arrange a follow-up review at no additional cost. Our goal is your success, not just a one-off audit.

How much does ISO certification cost?

Fees are fixed and transparent, calculated based on your organisation's size, number of sites, and chosen standard. We provide a full written quotation within 24 hours of your enquiry — no hidden fees, no surprises. Contact us to get your free, no-obligation quote.
