Common Questions

Family Law & Estate FAQ

Straightforward answers regarding mediation, asset distribution, and trusts.

In mediation, both spouses work cooperatively with a neutral attorney mediator to create mutually acceptable divorce terms in private. In litigation, disputes are submitted to a family court judge whose rulings are binding and public.
A will must go through probate court, which is public, time-consuming (often taking 9 to 18 months), and costly. A revocable living trust passes assets directly to beneficiaries immediately, in complete privacy, without probate court involvement.
New York follows 'equitable distribution', meaning marital assets are divided fairly (not necessarily 50/50). We work with forensic CPAs and business valuation appraisers to protect non-marital assets and ensure accurate division.
