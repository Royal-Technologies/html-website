Our Philosophy

Crafted Over Open Fire

At SteakCraft, every cut begins with rare USDA Prime and A5 Japanese Wagyu beef, dry-aged in our Himalayan salt-lined chambers for up to 45 days. Seared over white-oak embers and paired with vintage selections from our 700-label cellar, each evening is an ode to timeless hospitality.

The Artisans

Meet Our Chefs

Our culinary team brings decades of Michelin-pedigree expertise, passion, and artistry to every plate they create.

Marco Renaldi

Executive Chef & Founder

Renowned for his mastery of dry-aging and open-fire cookery, Chef Marco trained in Rome, Paris, and New York before founding SteakCraft in 2008.

Isabelle Dupont

Head Pastry Chef

A graduate of Le Cordon Bleu Paris, Chef Isabelle transforms seasonal ingredients into ethereal dessert sculptures that balance classic French technique with modern flair.

Hiroshi Tanaka

Sous Chef — Steak & Grill

With 15 years at Tokyo's finest Wagyu houses, Chef Hiroshi brings precision and reverence for premium beef to every cut he prepares.
