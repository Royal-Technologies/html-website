Artisanal Heritage

Artisanal Heritage, Modern Silhouette

Founded in 2001 in New York City, LuxeThread emerged from a singular conviction: that true luxury is not marked by loud logos, but by flawless drapery, tactile luxury, and responsible craftsmanship.

We partner with fourth-generation silk mills in Como, heritage woolen mills in Yorkshire, and eco-certified tanneries in Florence. Every seam is checked by hand before receiving our gold thread seal.
