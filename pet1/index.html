Why HappyPaws

A Stress-Free Experience For Cats & Dogs Alike

We know a trip to the vet can trigger anxiety in pets. That's why HappyPaws Clinic was architected from the ground up as an official Fear-Free Certified facility.

We provide separate cat and dog waiting lobbies, pheromone-diffused exam rooms, non-slip rubber tables, and lots of high-value liver treats to turn vet visits into a fun adventure!

  • Separate Cat & Dog Waiting Lounges
  • Calming Music & Soothing Natural Pheromones
  • Low-Stress Handling Techniques & Gentle Restraint
  • Transparent Pricing With Written Estimates Prior To Treatment
