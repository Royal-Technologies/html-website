Legal Retainer FAQ

Important information regarding case intake, fee models, and representation agreements.

During your initial consultation, a senior partner reviews your case facts, contracts, and relevant evidence under complete confidentiality. We identify immediate legal risks, assess dispute exposure, and provide strategic next steps.
We offer transparent fee models tailored to the matter: hourly billing with detailed itemized invoices, monthly flat-fee corporate general counsel retainers, and hybrid contingency structures for qualifying commercial claims.
For critical matters involving asset dissipation, trade secret theft, or contract breaches, our rapid-response litigation unit can draft and file an Order to Show Cause and Temporary Restraining Order (TRO) within 24 to 48 hours.
