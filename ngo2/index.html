Water Changes Everything

Breaking the Chains of Water Poverty

In rural communities, women and young girls walk an average of 6 kilometers every day carrying 40-pound jerry cans of contaminated surface water, keeping girls out of school and trapping families in perpetual hardship.

When a clean solar well is drilled in a village center, childhood diarrhea drops by 90%, school attendance spikes, and mothers have the time to cultivate crops and start small businesses.

  • 100% of public donations directly fund water project hardware
  • Local village committees trained with spare parts and maintenance budgets
  • Zero dry well rate: rigorous geophysical hydro-surveys conducted first
  • Every well documented with precise GPS coordinates and photos
Our Rigorous Protocol

How a Village Well is Born

A community-led, sustainable process that guarantees clean water for decades to come.

1

Geophysical Survey

Hydrogeologists map underground fractures to guarantee deep, permanent water flow.

2

Rotary Drilling

Industrial rotary rigs drill down to clean bedrock aquifers and install slotted steel casings.

3

Solar & Tower Build

Solar pumps, distribution tap stands, and elevated storage tanks are constructed.

4

Handover & IoT

Water quality certified, village committee trained, and IoT sensor activated.
